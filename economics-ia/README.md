# Macroeconomics commentary — Strait of Hormuz, scarcity and food-price inflation

**Research question:** To what extent will the scarcity of energy and fertiliser caused by
disruptions to the Strait of Hormuz contribute to global food-price inflation?

## A note on sources

gathered from search summaries of the FAO, World Bank, IMF and EIA releases. The primary
documents themselves were not reachable from this environment, so **verify these figures at
source before submitting.**
