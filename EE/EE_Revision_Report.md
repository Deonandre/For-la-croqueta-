### 1. "Almost no close reading of language"

Fixed, and this is the biggest change in the round. The essay now quotes and analyses Bryant's actual words.

**§3.1** was a paraphrase of three analogies. It is now a close reading of three quotations:

| Quotation | What the essay now analyses |
|---|---|
| "was like a wild horse that had the potential to become Secretariat" | The present/conditional split; how the verb phrase "had the potential to become" concedes the wildness while reclassifying it as unrealised quality; how the simile redistributes responsibility, since a horse is not accountable for its own training and Jackson is placed in the trainer's position |

**§3.2** now quotes "I have friends" and analyses the three-move sequence: admit the charge, supply a standard he fails, attribute the failure to a commitment rather than a defect.

**§3.3** is built around a block quotation from the press conference and analyses four features: the self-posed interrogative, the "simple"/"hard" antithesis, the pronoun shift from generic "yourself" to "I", and the contraction to three monosyllables after two long clauses.
