## B. Revised central argument

> The public image of the "Mamba Mentality" is neither Bryant's unmediated self-expression nor an invention imposed on him from outside. Because Bryant held an executive-producer credit on *Kobe Bryant's Muse* and is the film's only interviewee, the documentary cannot be set against his interviews as "media" against "self"; both carry his self-definition. The two text types therefore contribute **different kinds** of thing. The interviews supply the *content* of the philosophy — the analogies that carry it out of sport, the vocabulary of process, and a grammar that converts habits into identity. *Muse* supplies its *form*: associative chronology, the contrast between monochrome and archive, and Kris Bowers's score give those propositions coherence, emotional weight and an appearance of inevitability that Bryant's spoken words do not themselves possess. These formal operations were performed by the director, editors, cinematographers and composer, not by Bryant. The Mamba Mentality that circulates publicly is therefore best described as a **collaboratively mediated self-portrait**: largely Bryant's in content, substantially the filmmakers' in form, and belonging wholly to neither.

**What makes this a genuine "to what extent" answer:** it does not say "both contributed a lot." It says they contributed *unequally and in different registers*, it identifies which is necessary and which is amplifying, and it states what the method cannot reach (audience uptake).

## One thing I'd push back on

Your original essay's instinct — that the Mamba Mentality is constructed rather than simply expressed — was right, and it is the reason this revision had somewhere to go. The problem was never the instinct. It was that the evidence assembled to support it was partly unverifiable, and that the conclusion ("it was created by the media") overshot what that evidence could show.

The version you now have makes a **harder** claim than the original, not a softer one: that the self/media distinction your essay was organised around cannot be sustained at all. That is a more demanding argument, and it is better supported.
